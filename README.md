## About Me

Developer building AI-powered systems and full-stack web apps with Python and TypeScript. I like turning real-world problems into systems that are reliable, measurable and easy to maintain.

## Featured Projects

### [Permission-Aware AI Search](https://github.com/CCY-0124/casestudy_bookstack) · case study

Semantic search for a school wiki, live for 1,000+ daily users. Returns real pages filtered by the wiki's own permissions, never generated answers. Recall@5 0.91 on 79 labelled questions.

`Python` `Flask` `ChromaDB` `Gemini` `Docker Swarm`

### [Quant Trading Systems](https://github.com/CCY-0124/casestudy_quant_trading) · case study

A market-neutral funding-rate arbitrage bot across Bybit and IBKR, plus a long/short on-chain signal pipeline with walk-forward validation and a scheduled daily trading run. Dry-run by default and covered by pytest.

`Python` `pandas` `CCXT` `ib_insync` `pytest`

### [No Plan No Future](https://github.com/the-scholiast/NoPlanNoFuture)

Productivity and fitness platform with tasks, a calendar and workout tracking. Two-person team.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `Tailwind`

### [Scholiast](https://github.com/CCY-0124/Scholiast)

AI web app that turns PDFs and YouTube videos into quizzes and flashcards. Five-person team.

`Node.js` `Express` `MongoDB` `Gemini`
